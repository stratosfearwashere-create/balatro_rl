# Joker, voucher, tag, consumable and pack table (Balatro University Gold Stake notes, 177 videos)

Method notes. Source: the 18 files in notes/. Only lines of the form `- [area] IF ... THEN ...` inside a video section were counted as "rules" (2,750 such lines); the "Cross-video patterns" summary blocks were not counted. Counts are tallies made by reading each item's matching lines; "rules" excludes regex false positives and passing side-mentions, "bought/kept" and "skipped/sold" count rules whose headline verdict is take/keep or skip/sell/drop, and neutral rules (play tips, valuations, mixed take-then-sell, admitted mistakes) are counted only in "rules". Names follow the fixes given plus the notes' own header fixes (Banana and Michelle = Gros Michel, Slide Joker = Sly Joker, Burger = Burglar, Crypted = Cryptid, Emilate = Immolate). Unidentified names are kept as given and labelled (unidentified); "(?)" marks from the notes are kept. Counts for the multi-card consumable rows (Death, Mercury and similar) and the pack rows are approximate (a line that covers several cards is counted once per card). Video numbers are three-digit video numbers.

- Default is to avoid skipping: skipping loses a shop view (worth roughly $5-10, or $30 per 085) and sends you to the next boss with higher requirements; "skips are worth 50% more points" so the reward must justify ~50% harder blinds (039, 079, 145, 061, 065, 125, 151).
- Skip only if you can survive: check the next blind and boss first (e.g. 1350 vs 300 x4.5; 700 vs 300 with two five-card hands); better to roll, open packs and shop first so the skip is safe (051, 102, 128, 092, 093, 113, 111).
- Do not double-skip (two ante-1 tags) to the boss: too dangerous, take at most one; Black deck with no jokers is worst; he plays the small blind, shops, then decides (128, 162, 131, 076, 160).
- Which blind: play the small blind, skip the big blind, because you get the first shop (buffoon pack/value joker) before the big blind and still get the reward (128, 162); on Black deck open the first shop's buffoon pack before deciding (162).
- Deck matters: Yellow/Checkered/Plasma/Painted decks make skipping safer (4 hands, 600 reachable in one five-card hand); Black deck and Erratic are riskier; on Checkered at ante 1 he saves money for the voucher instead (162, 160, 042).
- Take the skip when a joker is needed first: boss The Hook with Investment tag only after taking a scoring joker (170); skip small tag for Negative if the shop has no jokers (156); early skip for a tag when no scoring jokers and the boss is rough (061).
- Skips matter more early and when tags are stacked: with double tags stacked, skips are worth far more; Standard tags early ~1 seal per mega pack, spectral fine at ante 4-5, Charm huge late (175, 068, 120). Economy not worth skipping on Anaglyph ante 1 (175).
- Skipping is wrong when money is already secure or the shop holds the value pieces (rental rebate income secure 172; Certificate + Green Joker 151; $22 with value generators, save for Paintbrush 033; Wee growth over money 105).
- Skipping is dangerous with Campfire (do not skip to boss; at ante 8 skip tags force boss skips) (074).
- Skip packs, not blinds, for Red Card when held (scales); take only a gold card/seal in a standard pack (001, 048, 068, 158). Context only.

## Sticker rules

### ETERNAL

**General stance and limits**
- Take an eternal that stabilizes antes 1-3 (flat +mult, foil/holographic +chips, Abstract, Mystic Summit, Smiley, Blue Joker, Matador, Banner). Late uselessness is fine; the only question is whether it carries early. One such "eternal not in the final build" is OK. (122, 131, 130, 147, 153, 170, 177, 064)
- Early eternal foil/holographic jokers are takeable even eternal; "two foils probably not". Eternal Sly/foil +50 chips accepted because few ways exist to stabilize early. (035, 147, 153, 177, 124, 043)
- One eternal value generator is fine and he "always" takes it in shop 1 (Golden, Trading Card, Business Card, Gift Card, To the Moon, Faceless, Astronomer, Certificate, Free Roll, Northern Lion). Never two. (131, 148, 027, 015, 145, 159, 046, 138, 128, 114, 100, 137)
- Never more than two value generators as eternals; two eternals leave no room for role players (scaling mult, chips, one flexible slot). Third eternal only if the first two are replaceable by endgame. (016, 011, 049, 130, 122, 055, 116)
- Eternal + rental on the same joker: skip, since both costs stack and cannot be shed. Eternal-only or rental-only is fine. (011, 013, 083, 085, 142, 166, 036, 013)
- Two eternal rentals together: do not take (Ancient Joker + Chad). Missing a first shop of two eternal rentals is destabilizing but not fatal. (025, 007)
- Exceptions where eternal+rental IS taken (it is the engine or cheap to pay for): Reserved Parking on Blue; Chad with Investment tag cash; Trading Card; Bloodstone; holographic Blue Joker; Midas Mask (holographic); holographic mult; Mail-In Rebate; Abstract when no mult. (001, 022, 025, 056, 038, 058, 116, 124, 167, 032)
- Free items get no discount: if an eternal/rental joker is rejected at full price, reject it at $0. (041)
- Gold-stake odds: about 30% of shop jokers are eternal (and ~30% perishable, ~30% rental), so clean jokers are rare. (037, 024, 134)
- Stickers cost slots: a Buffoon pack of only eternal/rental jokers is skipped; take a clean joker instead. Don't open early Buffoon packs on Checkered or Plasma (risk of rentals/eternals). (141, 030, 086, 109, 007)
- Competition (highest ante) goal: avoid early eternals since they cannot be swapped for stronger jokers, though some help jump-start. (135)

**Deck and slot conditions**
- Blue deck: eternal Parking is a yes (extra hand pays; won a multiplayer game with it). Yellow deck: more willing (Lustful, Hallucination, Brainstorm). Black deck's extra slot: accept eternal Business Card/Zany/Hasty-type more freely. (001, 022, 027, 119, 132, 009, 172)
- Painted (4 slots): commit less to eternals; refuses eternal Swashbuckler/Hallucination/Matador, accepts holographic Walkie-Talkie; steel cards can replace eternals. (063, 069, 101, 058, 094)
- Red deck: two early eternals acceptable (Abstract + Faceless) because they enable greed. (127)
- Golden Joker eternal is taken with Investment tag or Pants; eternal Business Card "yes every time" on Gold (~$6/round). (148, 130, 015)
- Eternal Gift Card is "eternal Egg but better"; it blocks using Ankh on it. (145, 110)

**Specific eternal refusals**
- Refuses: Misprint ("too many friends"), Flash Card, Pareidolia, Satellite at shop 1, Midas Mask late/with glass, Faceless Joker or Hallucination when no scoring, Seance (three eternals shop), eternal Dusk early, eternal Scholar, eternal Four Fingers, eternal Burnt Joker, Todd when holding 2 eternals, Parking (non-Blue). (018, 075, 138, 050, 135, 158, 153, 149, 087, 053, 043, 088, 116, 040, 129)
- Hand-specific eternals (Droll-type flush-only) skipped unless holographic; eternal Wee Joker only on Abandoned ante 3-4. (091, 149)
- Eternal Erosion not OK, but eternal generic +mult (Mystic Summit, Fist, Abstract) fine. (032)
- Eternal chips jokers get stuck (later planets supply chips); a stuck eternal Free Reroll/Supernova blocked swaps. (100, 155)
- Eternal Superposition "dubious"; less bad on Ghost deck with Hex. Fifth eternal avoided if Verdant Leaf boss possible, yet five was accepted once with Hallucination. (011, 035)
- Eternal Campfire support: avoid more eternals so slots stay free to feed it; with eternals it is fine to buy Campfire. (001, 065)

**Interactions (Hex, Madness, Dagger, Ankh)**
- Ceremonial Dagger cannot eat eternals; rerolls often yield only eternals/rentals; keep reroll money for fodder. Dagger itself good even eternal+Hex. (171, 136, 024, 054)
- Madness kills non-eternal jokers, so buying becomes eternal-only (~one third of shop jokers); take it only with blue seals/Telescope/planets or eternal fodder; also rich rerolling finds nothing usable. Rental Madness declined by one video, taken in another. (099, 134, 024, 064, 012)
- Hex copies of eternal: Hex never destroys, Ankh can; open Buffoon pack first so a target (even eternal) can be chosen. Hex on rental/perishable is avoided. (165, 073, 108, 095)
- Judgment on Gold gives a random non-eternal/perishable/rental joker. (159)

### RENTAL

  *General principles and stated limits*
  - Core test: a rental is fine if you can afford the fee and it has a defined role (usually winning in fewer hands or generating money); the question is "can I afford it", not "is it worth it". (010, 025, 036, 105, 134)
  - Rentals are cheaper than they look: if scoring is struggling you play extra hands anyway, which costs money. A rental that lets you win in fewer hands is often money-positive. (092, 126, 163, 068)
  - Fees are paid by interest plus leftover-hand money; Red Card rental was the MVP of one run via this. Rule of thumb: one rental ($3/round) is fine if you make $6-7 a round. (013, 068, 020, 163)
  - Free-item test: a joker rejected at full price (eternal/rental Mad Joker, Baron, Misprint) is rejected at $0 too, since slot and sticker cost matter, not price. (041)
  - Early rentals or weak jokers are structural for surviving ante 1-4, then pivot out. On Gold roughly 30% of jokers are perishable and 30% rental, so clean jokers are rare. (044, 037, 036, 172)
  - Rental fee is a second recurring cost: with a fee pending, skip optional Buffoon/Standard packs and hold cash. Fee "prevents momentum" and caps rerolls. (111, 134, 101, 071, 152, 095)
  - Rentals are scary only when you cannot find the missing pieces; with Fist + Banner + Hologram in hand a second rental was fine. (103)
  - Learn to be less picky: early eternals/rentals taken for stability ("that's how you win 40 in a row"); take what is offered for stability first, then value generators. (036, 044)
  - Skipping a first shop (e.g. two eternal rentals) is not automatically fatal but destabilizing. (007)
