LESSONS FROM OUR GAMES (version 13; each seen in at least 2 games, most supported first)
- Main-hand levels are the second engine (+6 levels is about +1.3 blinds). Treat the level targets as hard requirements.
- Choose the main hand by how often you can draw it with 2 discards: you should be able to draw it in 3 of 4 hands. How often you have played it does not decide the choice, unless you own Telescope (its pack planet goes to your most played hand). Your levels must also match the hand the round player really plays. If by ante 2 it mostly plays a different drawable hand (Two Pair played 5 times while Pair was 'the plan'), do one of two things: lock the style to the main hand, or level the hand it is playing (Uranus counts). Doing neither is the failure. Never flip the style back and forth between Pair and Two Pair, or between Pair and Straight.
- Pair is the default once the board has +Mult or chips jokers. It is always drawable, and Sly, Jolly, Duo, Half Joker, Hanging Chad + Photograph and Mercury support it. On a blank board (no jokers), Pair is not the plan; see the in-blind pace rule. Leave Pair only for a real signal: Spare Trousers, or a hand-type joker plus a steady supply of that hand's planets. If the other hand's planet is rare (Uranus for Two Pair), commit to it only if you also secure supply (Telescope, or Celestial packs opened from surplus). Otherwise stay on Pair. A run that committed to Two Pair, turned down Telescope and Celestial packs, and spent $22 on joker rerolls had Two Pair at level 2 in antes 5-6 while the unused Pair sat at 4. A single common +Mult joker for another hand is not a signal, and neither is one off-plan planet from a pack. Two Uranus bought because of a lone Mad Joker, and Square Joker pulling a run onto Two Pair, both starved Pair. If you buy a hand-conditional xMult (The Trio), change the style and the planets to match it, or skip it. An eternal Trio on an unthinned deck saw 16 Two Pairs for every 2 trips.
- Use Full House, Straight or Flush only with a real enabler. Never level Straight (Saturn) without Shortcut or Four Fingers.
- Commit at the first planet decision, by the end of ante 1-2 (by the first Buffoon pick at the latest), and make sure the round player actually plays that hand. Once the main hand is named, buy only its planets and jokers that score it. Do not let a joker (Runner, Square, Mad) pull plays toward another hand, or spread plays or levels over Full House, Two Pair, Three of a Kind and High Card. If the jokers you own really support a different hand, switch to that hand by ante 3 at the latest and stop buying the old hand's planets. Failures: Pair levelled but played 0 times by ante 4 while Clever, Uranus and Earth were bought for other hands. Ante 4 reached with Pair 1, Two Pair 2, Straight 4 and Flush 2. Four packs ($22) split levels over Uranus, Mercury and Venus, leaving Pair at 3 at ante 5.
- Take every planet for the main hand. Buy Mercury whenever it shows up, including in ante 1-2 shops; this is a budgeted spend, not something left to pack luck. Leaving a Celestial pack at $20 and passing planets at $22 in ante 1-2 meant Pair was still level 1 entering ante 3. Do not spend money on off-plan planets. Exception: when a pack you already opened (free or paid) has no main-hand planet, take the planet for a related hand (Two Pair, Three of a Kind, Full House; not Pluto) rather than nothing. That insures you against The Eye and The Mouth. Count it as a miss, not as progress, and never as a reason to open another pack.
- Targets: level 2-3 by the ante 2 boss (buy at least one main-hand planet before it), 3-5 by antes 3-4, 6-9 by antes 5-6. 'At target' means the target of the ante you are about to enter. With no xMult and the next ante below 30%, aim for level 5+ entering ante 5. Passing a Celestial pack at ante 4 because level 3 was 'inside 3-5', while ante 5 read 5-15%, left Pair at level 3 until death. Never let a second ante pass with the main hand at level 1. Once the hand is at target and the gap is a multiplier (about 2x score needed), planets stop counting as progress: a Celestial pack bought then is not a 'certain upgrade' (one at Pair 5 gave Venus). Send that $4 to a Buffoon pack or a reroll for xMult. While the board has no reliable xMult, do not keep building planet infrastructure for a hand already at target: $38 of Telescope and packs took Pair to level 8 while the outlook still showed a cliff.
- While below target: buy the main-hand planet on sight ($3-4). Take one Celestial look per shop before any joker reroll, and always buy the planet before a 2nd reroll in the same shop. In antes 3-4 below target, open every Normal Celestial pack you can afford above your floor. With $25 held, use the money above it on rerolls to find the main-hand planet before buying flat +Mult bridges. Pair stayed at level 2 through ante 3 with only three packs opened all run, while shops were left at $19 and $27. When the hand is 2+ levels below target, single planets, Celestial packs ($4) and Telescope all come before joker rerolls. On a thin board under $10, a $3-4 planet beats a reroll or a second Buffoon pack. Cap rerolls at about $10 per ante unless you hold $60+. Below level 3 at ante 3, or below level 4 at ante 4, planets also come before holding interest above $25. Below about $15 with no way to reach $25 within the next ante, do not pass Telescope, a Celestial pack or Mercury to protect a tier. Take a Meteor Tag when it comes free, but do not skip a blind in antes 2-4 just for one.
- Reroll budget: if $10 of rerolls in one ante has not found an xMult, put the rest of the surplus into main-hand planets until the hand reaches target. Levels are certain progress; rerolls are a lottery.
- Celestial packs: buy them only to level the main hand. Once the main-hand planet shows up in shops, buy it there for $3 rather than chasing it through Jumbo packs. Once two Celestial packs in a row miss the main-hand planet, stop buying them that ante and put the money into jokers or xMult rerolls: five packs for about $22 gave Jupiter and four Uranus. While below target, open a Celestial pack if you keep $20+ after it, or about $10-15 once $25 is covered, and nothing better is on offer. A voucher that fixes the run (Director's Cut) always comes first. Prefer packs to rerolls and to $6+ flat jokers and slow scalers. Buy Normal packs, not Mega, unless 2+ planets in the Mega pack are on-plan. Never open one that leaves you unable to fix the coming blind.
- If the main hand's planets have not shown up after two antes (by ante 3), switch to a drawable hand whose planets you keep being offered. If Pair jokers are on the board, that means Pair. If packs have already levelled another drawable hand while the main hand sits at level 1, set the style to that hand so the levels get used.
- Telescope and Planet Merchant pay off only when about 3+ Celestial packs will follow them, or with Constellation. When that is the plan and the main hand is committed, buy Telescope in antes 2-4 even if it costs one interest tier. Do not buy it for a hand already at target while the board still lacks xMult. Below target with no pack plan, spend that $10 on direct main-hand planets first. [seen in 42 games]
- Reroll limits. Two failures recur: chaining rerolls in one shop, and never rerolling while a cliff approaches. The limits below depend on what the outlook shows for the NEXT ante.
- At most 2 rerolls per shop (stop before a $7 reroll) unless you hold $60+. Spread them across shops, where each costs $5. Never chain escalating rerolls ($5, $6, $7, $8) in one shop. Pay for a joker on offer rather than for more looks at the shop. If the main hand is below target and a Celestial pack (even a Mega) is in the shop, buy it before a third reroll. Starting ante 6 at $31, a missed Celestial plus rerolls at $5, $6 and $7, passing a Mega Celestial twice, left $9 with a rental due and no money for later shops. Four rerolls plus a Banner took a safe ante 4 from $39 to $12, and ante 5 was played on $3-7.
- When rerolling to save a failing blind, drop at most one interest tier per shop, and buy the certain upgrades already in the shop first (main-hand planet, Celestial pack). In ante 2, $34→$14 and $25→$14 of rerolls found only an eternal conditional Hack and a Bootstraps rental that the strategist then felt forced to buy.
- Set your buy bar before you reroll. If two rerolls in a row show only cheap scorers you pass on, the bar is too high: lower it and buy the best scorer instead of rerolling again. Nine rerolls ($51 over antes 3-4) once bought no joker at all.
- Never reroll if you could not then buy a $6-8 xMult on top of your floor. If two or three shops in a row turn up nothing, stop rerolling. Put the surplus into main-hand planets or rebuild money so the xMult can be bought when it appears.
- Antes 1-2: never reroll when it takes money below about $15. While one or two shops remain before the boss, do not reroll below $20 either. A low boss read early in the ante is not yet an emergency, so buy the cheap scorer on offer now. When the current ante reads 95-100%, do not reroll below $25: $27→$22 at a 98% next blind gave up a tier for nothing. In ante 1 at 95%+ with under $20, do not reroll at all: a reroll at $15 (99%/95%) found only a Duo it did not buy. Exceptions: a single emergency reroll on an empty board before a blind (never chain a second one); and with 2+ empty slots while the next ante's boss reads below 30%, one $5 reroll per shop down to $15, after buying any lasting scorer on offer. Leave ante 1 with $10-15 so the ante 2 shops can buy two scorers.
- With a rental, keep at least rent + $4 after any reroll. In antes 1-2, never spend $15+ rerolling one shop.
- Once 2 lasting scorers exist and the next ante does NOT show a cliff: never reroll below $20 unless the next blind or the current ante's boss reads below 70%. While every blind of the current ante reads 95%+, never reroll below $25 ($28 with a rental), and do not reroll below the next $5 tier.
- Cliff exception (antes 3-5): when the next ante's boss reads about 15% or less, or its Small or Big Blind about 30-40% or less, and the board has no xMult, the $25 floor stops applying to xMult rerolls in the current safe ante. Do 1-2 $5 rerolls in EACH shop of that ante (after the certain upgrades in the shop) down to a floor of about $15 (+$3 per rental). Never go under $10 that way, and never chain a 3rd reroll in one shop. Refusing a $5 reroll at $27 'because $22 breaks the floor' while ante 5 read 35/5/0, then spending $16 in one ante 5 shop when the Small was already 34%, was too late. So was leaving ante 3-4 shops at $20-27 with zero rerolls while the next ante read 0-15%, then spending $29 on rerolls in ante 5.
- Otherwise, from ante 3, reroll only with money above $25 ($28 with a rental, $31 with two), and only if you can open a slot: know which joker you would sell before you reroll. Stop once one shop has used about $20 on rerolls. Never pay $8-10 for a reroll unless you hold $60+.
- If the main hand is below its level target, take a Celestial look first and cap rerolls at about $10 per ante.
- Buy money-scaling jokers (Bull, Bootstraps) only if you will really hold $25+, and never under $20. Do not buy them on a plan that spends heavily on rerolls or rentals, or while the engine is still being bought. A core Bootstraps made the strategist refuse a boss counter, and the run lost to The Club. While you own one, check the next-blind outlook after each spend. Stop if it would fall below about 85% or drop more than 5 points.
Never rerolling at all is also a failure. A run that rerolled once all game, leaving shops at $14-22 with empty slots while the next ante read 0-25%, lost. While the board has fewer than 2 multipliers, use 1-2 $5 rerolls per shop with money above the floor, in the early shops of the ante rather than the boss shop. If the boss itself is the problem, first look for a fix aimed at that boss. [seen in 33 games]
- Rentals and debt. A rental's real price is $3 times the rounds you will keep it (about 20 rounds from ante 2, so about $15-20+ even for a $1 rental), not its sticker. Before buying one, money after the purchase should be at least $3 × the rounds until you could sell it, plus $5. A rental also raises the money floor to $28, which blocks rerolls for the rest of the run.
Antes 1-2: buy no rentals, even at $1 or as a free pack pick, unless the current blind fails without one. Never take a defensive rental that adds no score (Mr. Bones): if the boss reads 60% or below, take the scoring option even off-plan, or skip the pick. A rental Mr. Bones at ante 2 cost $3 a round for about 4 rounds, sold for $1, and only hid a board too weak for The Fish. The other ante 1 exceptions:
- The board has no scoring joker and either the ante's boss reads below about 90% or ante 2's Small Blind reads below about 50%. Then a rental that scores now (e.g. Half Joker on Pair) beats a scaler that starts at 0.
- You hold an xMult with no base. Then a big flat-chips rental (Stuntman +250) or an affordable Bootstraps is worth it.
Ante 2: buy a rental only if the current blind reads below about 50% and no non-rental option fixes it, or under the lasting-scaler exception below. Antes 3-4: buy one only if the next blind reads below 70% without it, or under one of the exceptions. Carry at most one rental in antes 2-4. A flat scoring rental bought while the ante reads 90%+ is never worth it. Never take a rental for flat Mult when a non-rental fix exists. Never take a money-scaling rental (Bull, Bootstraps) in antes 1-4 unless you will really keep $28+, even when it is free. Never take a new rental, or a second one, below $20 (except the multiplier exception below). Bootstraps (eternal rental) bought at $14 and Shoot the Moon (eternal rental, no Queens in the deck) at $9 left $8 against $6 a round of rent, and every later buy lowered the boss read. A Half Joker rental at $6 followed by Ramen as a second rental at $9 went into -$5 debt. From ante 3, one lasting rental that is a core scorer for the main hand is acceptable only with a free slot and only if you still hold $28 after buying it. If a second rental is the only survival fix, sell one at the first shop where the next blind reads 85-90%+ without it, before money hits $0. Never hold two rentals below $28, and never carry two across an ante below $31.
Lasting-scaler exception (Yellow Deck): from ante 2, with $25+ in hand and the next boss safe, one rental that is a lasting xMult or a scaler you will really feed (Hologram; Green Joker played with zero discards; Supernova on Pair) is acceptable. Hold $28 afterwards.
Multiplier exception: from ante 2, if the board has no xMult and an upcoming boss reads below 50%, an affordable rental xMult that fires on every main-hand play (Cavendish, Card Sharp on a repeated hand, Baseball with Uncommons, Acrobat, Vampire) beats any flat +Mult option. From ante 3 with no xMult, a multiplier enabler counts too (Blueprint or Brainstorm copying a grown scorer), even below $20: buy it and rebuild to $28 by skipping optional spends. Declining a $1 Blueprint (eternal rental) at $16, which would have copied a +32 Spare Trousers or a +92 Square, left a Polychrome Banner as the run's only multiplier. Accept an eternal rental only for such an every-hand multiplier when nothing sellable is available. Never use the exception for a periodic or conditional xMult. Refuse it too if you already carry a rental.
Eternal rentals: never buy one for anything except that every-hand multiplier. It charges $3 every round for the rest of the run and locks a slot. A $1 eternal rental Banner kept money at $1-17 for the rest of the run. An eternal rental Riff-Raff bought as an empty-board patch paid rent for about 11 rounds, did nothing once the board was full, and blocked an xMult slot in ante 5.
Always:
- With a rental, the floor is $28, not $25. Never spend to $0 while holding one.
- Never use Credit Card or debt to pay for scoring in antes 1-3. At $0 or below, buy no rentals.
- Never take a rental that is perishable or decaying (Popcorn, Ice Cream, Ramen), even as a free pack pick. If you hold one, sell it the shop before it expires: a perishable rental Mystic Summit taken on an empty board at ante 1 kept charging $3 a round after it was debuffed. Never carry a third rental.
- Sell a survival rental as soon as the blind you rented it for is behind you, unless the outlook shows you still need it.
- Do not let the $28/$31 floor block buys that improve the build.
- Before a boss that costs money (The Tooth), enter with cash for the cards you expect to play + $3 per rental + $5. [seen in 30 games]
- Interest against forecasts (once 2 lasting scorers exist; before that, the survival rule applies). Survival overrides interest for a failure the outlook predicts in the NEXT ante too, not only for the next blind: $5 of interest is worth nothing if the run dies one ante later. But a cliff is fixed by steady, targeted spending in the safe ante's shops, never by emptying the bank in one shop.
- No cliff ahead: hold $25 once the board already passes the next ante. Drop below $25 ($28 with a rental) for survival only when the next blind or the current ante's boss reads below about 70%. A mildly bad next-ante forecast is normal in antes 2-5 and on its own never justifies spending down every ante. A high outlook that rests on a variable joker, or a boss that hides information, is no reason to spend the bank either. While the current ante reads 95%+ in antes 2-4, do not buy speculative packs, off-plan jokers whose trigger the main hand never makes, or random Arcana that take you below $25. Do not open a pack unless you will take a pick from it, and never buy a $4 pack just to skip it and feed Red Card (+3 Mult for $4 is a poor upgrade; about $36 of packs, many skipped, grew Red Card to only +15). $27 went to $11 in ante 2 on a Buffoon pack with every card skipped, a perishable Superposition on a Two Pair plan, and a random Wheel of Fortune, and antes 3-4 ran at $3-14. From ante 4, while the current ante reads 95%+ and no cliff is shown, spend only the surplus above $25 ($28 with a rental) and never drop below $20.
- Moderate cliff (next ante's boss below about 50%): spend down to the $25 tier in every shop of the current ante on certain upgrades.
- Severe cliff (next ante's boss about 15% or less, its Small or Big Blind below about 30-40%, any next-ante blind below 20%, the outlook at 20% or less for two shops in a row, or the board's only xMult is perishable and expires within the next ante), especially with no xMult. A cliff one ante away is an emergency now, not when you reach it. The interest floor no longer applies. In EACH shop of the current ante that still has a blind after it, add at least one certain upgrade: a main-hand planet, a Celestial pack while below target, a Buffoon pack, or a lasting scorer for the played hand in an empty slot. If the main hand is 3+ levels below target, related-hand planets and Planet Merchant count too. Then use 1-2 $5 xMult rerolls per shop (see the reroll rule's cliff exception). Spend down to a floor of about $15 (+$3 per rental), never under $10 unless the next blind itself reads below 70%, and aim to enter the cliff ante with roughly $15-25. Failures on both sides: runs that left ante 3-4 shops at $19-37 with no reroll 'to keep the tier' while the next ante read 0-15% (passing Uranus, Mars, Square Joker, Red Card and Zany too) then spent $28-29 in a panic in the cliff ante, down to $4, too late to matter. A run that put four rerolls and a Banner into one safe-ante shop ($39→$12) and then a Mega Buffoon ($12→$4) played the cliff ante on $3-7 with no money to hunt xMult.
- If the outlook has shown the next ante at 0-10% for two antes in a row, the build lacks a multiplier. If three shops in a row miss, stop rerolling and rebuild to the price of an xMult ($6-10).
Spend only on things that directly add xMult, main-hand levels or, in an empty slot, score on the played hand. Spectral and random Arcana packs and one-card tarots do not count as fixing a forecast, except an Arcana aimed at a named edit on trigger cards (feeding a conditional xMult, see the multiplier rule). Do not dump the money into escalating rerolls in one shop. A joker whose value needs money every ante (Campfire, reset at each boss) is not a fix when you are broke.
When the boss is below 50% and you dip into survival money, judge each $ by how much it raises the boss estimate. Prefer a main-hand planet already in the shop, then joker rerolls. If two buys in a row miss, stop and keep the interest. [seen in 28 games]
- Unreliable scoring is backup, not the plan. That includes perishable jokers (Space, Banner, Blue Joker, Bootstraps, Brainstorm, Wrathful when perishable), decaying ones (Ice Cream, Popcorn, Seltzer, Turtle Bean), random ones (Misprint), time-limited ones (Seltzer, Ramen) and destroyable ones (Gros Michel). Every one you hold is a slot that must be refilled later. From ante 3, pick a lasting scorer even if it scores less right now. A board of Space, Popcorn, Banner and Wrathful lost all four by ante 4 and was left with three weak jokers.
- From ante 2, do not spend $5+ on perishable or flat +Mult jokers while the current ante already reads 100%. Put that money into main-hand planets, Buffoon packs for xMult or scalers, or interest. A perishable Bootstraps at $7 bought while The Psychic read 100% expired before it mattered. A Banner bought at a 100% ante was debuffed by ante 5.
- When the outlook shows a later blind at 0-10% and the board has no xMult, the cause is a missing multiplier. Do not patch the next blind with perishables or decaying jokers; they expire together and leave the board empty. Blue Joker (perishable, Jolly sold for it) and Popcorn were bought while ante 4-5 read 0-10%. By ante 5 Blue was debuffed, Popcorn was at +4, and the Small Blind fell to 12%.
- Count a perishable as a scorer only for the blinds it will still be alive for, usually the current ante. Count Seltzer as gone once its hands run out. If it expires before the first failing blind, the problem is not solved: keep searching for a permanent xMult and keep money ready for it.
- When a perishable xMult is the board's main multiplier, count its rounds on the day you take it. Plan its replacement to arrive at least one shop before expiry. Never 'keep interest' while a perishable carries the board.
- Hold at most 2 perishables at once. Once 2 are on the board, the next joker bought must be a lasting one. Never replace an expiring perishable with another perishable, or sell a lasting scorer to buy one (a permanent +8 Jolly sold for Seltzer, $14→$9), unless the current blind fails without it.
- When checking a boss two or more blinds ahead, count a decaying joker at what it will be worth by then and a perishable that expires first as zero. By ante 3, have at least 2 scoring jokers that do not shrink. If a bridge is the only fix, buy it, but the next spend goes to something lasting, and the bridge's slot is the first to replace.
- Do not pay $5 for a shrinking joker when it leaves you under $5, unless the current blind fails without it.
- Never let two perishables expire in the same ante or right at a boss.
- When the danger is 1-2 antes out and the current ante reads 80%+, two cases apply:
  (a) Do not churn stopgaps. Stopgaps bought and sold one shop later drained $50 to $18. Do not let them take the money or slot meant for xMult, main-hand planets or deck shaping.
  (b) With an empty slot and the next ante below about 30%, do not turn down a joker that scores on the played hand only because of its sticker, within the 2-perishable cap. Buy it if it will still be alive at the failing blind.
- Replace a shrinking chips joker before it falls to about half its value.
- From ante 3, treat a flat joker that scores under 10% of the hand as a slot to free.
- If more than half the board's scoring is unreliable, work out the bad case and keep buying lasting scoring until the boss clears without those pieces.
- Never let a perishable or rental joker be your only chips source. [seen in 24 games]
- Eternal jokers are a permanent slot cost. Before any Eternal buy, ask: 'when the xMult shows up, which slot does it go in?' The sticker only costs something if you will need that slot later.
- Accept an Eternal sticker only on an xMult or an unconditional scorer or scaler for the main hand that you would keep to ante 8. Never on a conditional joker (Hack), a joker for another hand type (Crazy Joker on a Pair plan), a joker whose trigger you lack (Shoot the Moon without Queens), a non-scoring joker (Riff-Raff, economy, utility) or a rental economy joker (Bootstraps). Four Eternals (Hack, Bootstraps, Half Joker, Shoot the Moon) left only Greedy sellable by ante 4. An eternal Riff-Raff bought for $6 at $13 produced only Crafty, Drunkard, Misprint and Shoot the Moon (three sold for $2-3), forced three sales to afford The Duo, ruined Stencil, and was still a dead slot at ante 5.
- With 2+ empty slots and no scaler on the board, an Eternal scaler that grows on every main-hand play (Supernova, Ride the Bus, Green Joker on Pair) is worth its sticker; with a rental too, only if you can carry the rent while holding $28. Refusing Supernova with 3 empty slots (Pair was then played 20+ times) and Ride the Bus 'for being eternal', then taking eternal Raised Fist and an eternal rental Sly in desperation, lost the scaling.
- Before ante 5, lock at most one slot with a non-xMult Eternal, and only for a scaler or scorer that fits the main hand and does not fight the rest of the board. Never hold more than 2 Eternals that are not xMult at any point. Eternal Crazy (off-hand) plus eternal Red Card left two of five slots locked into flat Mult from ante 3.
- From ante 3, while the board has no xMult, keep at least one slot free or sellable for it. Buy no new non-xMult Eternal then. Never sell a key Mult source for an Eternal utility joker.
- On a near-empty board (3+ free slots) with a failing forecast before ante 4, the first Eternal scorer for the played hand costs little: do not turn it down for the sticker.
- Never take an Eternal flat or conditional +Mult or chips joker, or an Eternal utility joker, if it would make 2+ Eternal slots, even when it is free from a pack. The only exception is a next blind below 50% without it, with nothing else that fixes it.
- Keep at least 2 non-eternal slots you can sell or swap through ante 4 and beyond, so the convert-to-xMult step stays possible.
- If a blind forces a third Eternal, take the one with the highest ceiling (an xMult, Hanging Chad, a retrigger), not flat Mult.
- In antes 1-3, do not lock a slot with an Eternal joker that scores nothing or is flat filler (Riff-Raff, Shortcut, Four Fingers, Splash, Eternal plain Joker, Sly, Arrowhead, Fibonacci, Blue Joker). Take one only if the next blind is below 70% without it. An Eternal Shortcut taken as the first joker on an empty ante 1 board led the strategist to refuse The Duo and Clever as 'off the Straight plan'. The run went to Pair anyway with Straight at level 1, and by ante 4 Eternals filled every free slot.
- When opening a Buffoon pack with no jokers, rank the choices by how many chips they add to an ordinary hand right now, and take a scorer if there is one. If the pack has no scorer, take nothing in antes 1-2. Later, with 4+ free slots, an economy joker is acceptable only if no scorer will come to depend on it and you sell it for the first scorer.
- Do not buy a Buffoon pack, or skip for a Buffoon Tag, when every slot is full and the only sellable joker is a key scorer. [seen in 21 games]
- In antes 1-2, survival beats interest until two conditions hold: the board has 2 lasting, unconditional scoring jokers (+Mult or chips that fire on ordinary hands), and the main hand is level 2 or higher. Survival means every blind of the current ante, including its boss. Treat a boss reading below about 85% as a failing blind, even if the next blind reads 97%. Also judge ante 1 buys by the ante 2 boss read, not only by the next blind. When the current blind already reads 95%+ and the boss is safe too, do not pay for perishable, decaying or off-plan jokers, packs or rerolls. Until those 2 scorers are in place:
- Do not leave a shop holding $15+ while a blind of this ante is failing. Spend down to about $10. In ante 1, with fewer than 2 unconditional scorers, do not leave a shop at $12+ without buying an affordable unconditional +Mult or chips joker. A suit-conditional joker (Greedy) does not count. Before the main hand is fixed, a scorer for the hand the round player really plays (Clever while Two Pair is being played) is acceptable. Leaving ante 1 shops at $14 and $23 with only Greedy gave a 5% Flint. In ante 1, go below $10 only if a blind of this ante would fail, or if the next ante's Small Blind already reads below about 30%. In that case buy direct scorers or main-hand planets down to about $5-8. Never finish ante 1 under $10 otherwise.
- Direct scorers first. In antes 1-3, buy the cheapest certain scorer, check the outlook again, and pay for packs or rerolls only from money above $15. Never let one shop take a $20+ bank under $10 on gambles. $22 went to $3 on a reroll and two Celestial packs that missed; the next shop's $4 Green Joker took The Head from 15% to 94%, and money stayed at $1-11 for the rest of the run.
- Take a joker that scores now over a scaler that starts at 0 (e.g. Red Card), even if the scorer is a rental (see the rentals rule).
- An early conditional xMult (The Idol, The Duo) is a finisher, not a base. Until there is one unconditional +Mult or chips joker or a levelled main hand, rank cheap unconditional scorers and main-hand planets above conditional or perishable jokers.
- Cheap +Mult stopgaps (Jolly, Raised Fist, Gros Michel, or any perishable) are a bridge only when the current blind fails without them. Otherwise take a non-perishable scorer even if it is slightly weaker. Never let two stopgaps expire in the same ante or right at a boss.
- Packs: use the Yellow Deck head start only as part of a plan to reach $25 by ante 3. The extra $10 pays for one early pack, not a pack plus a reroll plus a buy. Below $20, open at most one pack per ante in antes 1-2, and none once the ante's blinds already read 95%+. In ante 1, open one only if you could still afford a shop joker or a reroll after a miss. A $4 Buffoon pack with every card skipped plus a $6 Jumbo whose Gluttonous was sold four decisions later took $18 to $10 while ante 1 read 95-97%, and money stayed under $15 until ante 4.
- Every joker slot and pack pick goes to scoring. Do not take utility, defensive or economy jokers (Drunkard, Chaos, Splash, Business Card, Hallucination, Mail-In Rebate, To the Moon, Cloud 9, Certificate, Mr. Bones, Riff-Raff) or a $10 voucher like Grabber. In antes 1-2 a Buffoon pack with no scorer is a skip, so take nothing rather than a free non-scorer. A free Drunkard became a crutch that Banner later depended on. A rental Mr. Bones taken over a scorer at ante 2 cost $3 a round and only hid that the board could not score The Fish.
- Zero scoring jokers before any blind from the ante 1 Big Blind on is an emergency. Buy any affordable scorer. If there is none, do one $5 reroll, then open an Arcana pack (Judgement). Never enter a blind with an empty board while holding $10+.
- With no scoring jokers, play for the biggest base hand you can draw (see the in-blind pace rule) and take pack jokers that pay off now (Runner over Certificate).
- An early xMult (e.g. Card Sharp) must be followed at once by an unconditional base joker and a main-hand planet.
- Take a value generator only when the next boss is safe by the conservative check.
Once the 2 lasting scorers are in place, bank: aim for $25 by the end of ante 2, and hold $25 from ante 3 (subject to the forecast rule). [seen in 20 games]
- From ante 2 to ante 5, while the board has fewer than 2 multiplier sources, spend all surplus above $25 in every shop (more under a severe cliff; see the forecast rule). Loyalty Card and other periodic xMult do not count as a source. Spending means 1-2 $5 rerolls spread across shops, Buffoon packs, and xMult or +Mult jokers. Start the hunt in earnest at ante 3, and keep the cash to buy the xMult when it appears. Do not spend the run's money on rerolls in a safe ante 2. If the main hand is below its level target, buy its planet first. Once the hand is at target, more planets, planet infrastructure and hand-boosting +Mult are not progress, so all surplus goes to rerolls and Buffoon packs for xMult until one reliable every-hand xMult is on the board.
- Never pass a real xMult (The Idol, Cavendish, Card Sharp, Acrobat and similar) just to keep an interest tier while a slot is open, or because of a rental or eternal sticker. Empty slots never grow. In antes 1-3, buy any affordable xMult that fits the plan even if it drops a tier, as long as you keep about $8-10. Bloodstone refused at $19 on a Pair deck 'because Hearts are only 13 cards' was followed by no usable xMult until ante 4.
- Conditional xMult (Bloodstone, Ancient, Blackboard, Flower Pot, Seeing Double) is worth buying when nothing better is on offer, but it is not 'the lasting multiplier' until the deck lets it fire on most hands. Count a suit-dependent xMult as half, and as nothing on an even 13-per-suit deck playing Pairs. If the outlook does not improve after buying it, it is not firing. Feed it on purpose in every following shop: tarots and Arcana packs that convert or remove off-suit cards (World, Moon, Sun, Lovers, Hanged Man, Sigil) come before generic rerolls, and keep hunting a true every-hand xMult. A Blackboard called 'my lasting xMult' with 26 of 52 dark cards left ante 6 at 0-15% for all of ante 5, while an Arcana Jumbo was passed twice and $29 went on rerolls; the one World used lifted The Plant from 87% to 95%.
- With empty slots in antes 2-3, buy durable, non-rental scorers that fit the main hand (Raised Fist, Sly, Castle or Greedy when the deck feeds them) with money above $25. With 2+ empty slots and the next ante below 30%, the first affordable lasting unconditional scaler or +Mult (Red Card, Fortune Teller, Jolly on Pair) qualifies even below $25: a slow scorer beats an empty slot. Passing Red Card at $22 with 3 empty slots 'because it starts at 0', then Fortune Teller, and leaving with no buy lost a run. Jokers for another hand type (Zany on Pair) still fall under the on-plan rule. A full board buys time to find the multiplier and prevents panic rental buys at ante 4. If the next ante reads below 30%, a perishable or eternal scorer for the played hand also qualifies; leaving 2-3 slots empty through ante 3 is a failure.
- If the board still has no xMult by ante 5, sell spent flat or economy jokers early to fund the search.
- Do not swap one flat +Mult or chips joker for another as a fix. It only counts if the outlook rises.
- Joker Stencil is a stopgap: take any xMult, scaling or growing joker even if it lowers Stencil.
- Do not spend this surplus on Arcana, Spectral or Standard packs that add one-card tweaks, or on economy, unless you are aiming at a specific card the build needs (such as feeding a conditional xMult).
- Spend a windfall of $20+ over target this way before the Small Blind, spread across that ante's shops. [seen in 18 games]
- Buy only jokers that fire on what you actually play and own. Before turning a joker down, check its condition against the main hand.
- Fix the main hand (Pair by default) by the first Buffoon pick. From then on, buy only scorers for that hand or unconditional ones. No jokers for a different hand type (Droll/Crafty for Flush, Crazy or Devious for Straight, Zany for Three of a Kind, Clever for Two Pair on a Pair plan) as a bridge or filler, and never as an Eternal. An eternal Crazy Joker bought on a Pair plan to pass The Needle saw Straight played twice all run and locked a slot. Buy unconditional +Mult or chips instead (Abstract, Banner, Popcorn; Jolly and Half Joker when Pair is the main hand), or reroll to find one. Not Blackboard, a conditional xMult that needs held Spades/Clubs.
- This applies to conditional xMult too. Do not buy The Order, The Tribe, The Family and similar for a hand you are not committed to or cannot draw reliably. Do not buy jokers whose trigger the main hand never makes (Superposition on Two Pair).
- Even for a committed hand, do not stack 3+ flat jokers that fire only on one hand type without an xMult. Before taking a hand-specific scaler at ante 2, check that the hand can reach the ante 5-6 level targets (planet supply) and that a slot stays free for an xMult. Spare Trousers, Clever and an eternal Square by the end of ante 2 locked a Two Pair board into flat scoring, and later xMult finds needed sales.
- Jokers tied to face cards or ranks (Photograph, Scary Face) fire only on some Pairs. No jokers that need cards you lack (Stone Joker with no Stone cards, Shoot the Moon with no Queens). Early on, skip conditional support.
- Early on, buy jokers that scale, multiply or add plain +Mult. Prefer one that scores now over a scaler starting at 0. If the shop has none, keep the money rather than filling slots with conditional or off-hand jokers.
- Buy for the smaller side: score ≈ (hand chips + joker chips) × (hand mult + joker mult) × xMult. Compare chips to mult before every +Mult buy. If chips are about 10 times mult or more, the next buy must be +Mult or xMult. Once Mult is about equal to or above chips (a Pair board at ~50 Mult on ~55 chips kept buying Gros Michel and Jolly), buy chips, levels or xMult instead. From ante 5, a board with 2+ +Mult jokers and an xMult but no chips joker, scoring under about 200 chips on the main hand, ranks a lasting chips source above another +Mult or planet.
- If you keep a scaler with a trigger you control, feed it on purpose, but from ante 3 only with money that stays at $25+. If you won't feed it, don't keep it while you lack scoring.
- No sideways swaps. Do not sell a flat or conditional joker to buy another of the same kind. From ante 3, do not buy flat or perishable fillers that only nudge a failing boss estimate, unless that joker alone lifts the next blind above about 80%.
- Do not hold scalers that pull against each other on discards (Banner, Green Joker, Mystic Summit want zero discards; Castle wants many; Ramen also wants zero, since it loses xMult per discarded card).
- Do not play Straights or Flushes as filler once you have jokers for the main hand and no enabler. In a desperate last shop, do not switch to a hand you can't reliably draw. The empty-board case is the exception: see the in-blind pace rule. [seen in 17 games]
- Skipping blinds. A skip gives up the blind payout, the unused-hand money, an interest round and a whole shop. Uncommon, Holographic, Foil, Voucher, Coupon, Polychrome, Negative and money tags pay out only after a later blind, so skipping sends you into the next blind with the same board.
- Do not skip in an ante where the boss is below about 40-50%, or where the boss estimate is likely optimistic. Never skip the Big Blind when the boss after it reads under 50%. Never skip both blinds of an ante into a boss below 50%. When you are broke (under about $10), play the blind for its shop and the unused-hand money.
- Skip a Small Blind only when the board reads about 100% through the next boss and money is at least $20.
- While the board has no xMult and the next ante reads below about 30%, never skip for an economy tag; every shop is a chance at the multiplier. In that state, skip only for a tag that shows jokers or planets at once (Buffoon, Charm), or for a Rare Tag as described next, and only if a slot is open or you will sell a named joker to take what it offers.
- Rare Tag exception: when the next ante's boss reads 10% or less with no xMult, the current ante's remaining blinds read 90%+ and a joker slot is open, a Rare Tag is the best xMult source available; skip a non-boss blind for it. This matters most for the Big Blind of the ante before the cliff, so the free Rare arrives in the first shop of the cliff ante. Playing the ante 4 Big Blind 'because the playbook says not to skip for Rare tags' while ante 5 read 35/5/0, The Mouth read 95% and three slots were open kept a $15 shop that brought only Even Steven.
- In antes 1-2, skip for an economy tag only on a solid board (at least 2 lasting scorers, or 1 unconditional scorer plus a main-hand planet) and a boss reading 98%+. In antes 2-4, do not skip for a Meteor or other planet tag.
- Later, skip the pre-boss blind for a money tag only if the boss has read at least 90% for two or more shops in a row and the shop you give up has nothing you want.
- Never skip for a D6 or other reroll tag while the board lacks scoring or the next ante's boss reads low.
- Never skip for a joker tag (Rare, Uncommon, Buffoon, Mega Buffoon, Holographic, Foil) with all slots full unless you have already named a weak non-eternal joker to sell. Once you have skipped, always take what it offers if it scores better or adds a multiplier: sell the weakest or expiring joker (spent Seltzer, a Banner with 1-2 rounds left, decaying Ice Cream, economy, flat filler) to make room. Take a free Blueprint or Brainstorm next to an xMult. If the outlook says a copy joker lowers the boss, treat that as an estimator error (copying an x2 cannot score less than the flat joker it replaces) and take it unless the next blind falls below about 70%. Turning down a free Blueprint to keep a Banner with 1 round left, which was then sold anyway, wasted the skip and its shop. When the run is struggling, skip only if the tag itself fixes the next blind or the coming cliff.
- When a free pack opens (Meteor, Mega Celestial), always take something: main-hand or Pair planets first, otherwise a related hand's planet. [seen in 16 games]
- Judge a board by its reliable scoring. The outlook is simulated with the boss's effect and the build as it is, but it still averages over lucky draws, and it can glitch.
- When reaching the target depends on a conditional joker (suit, rank or hand-type jokers, Flower Pot, Idol, Ancient, Castle, Blackboard) or on unreliable pieces, treat the boss as less safe than shown. If the whole board is one conditional joker, read 95% as about 85%. Lower it further when the boss debuffs cards or cuts hand options.
- Against bosses that change how the round is played (The Water with 0 discards, The Needle, The Psychic, The Mouth), treat the shop-phase read as optimistic. Ask for at least a 10-point margin, count hands and estimate by hand what you can score under the boss's rule, and buy survival scoring in the last shop if the margin is thin. The Water read 85-97% in the shop and 55% once the round began, on a board that needed to dig for Two Pair or trips.
- Before the boss, plan on the lowest recent read for that boss in the ante, not the latest one. If any read this ante was below about 75%, spend in the last shop on something that counters the boss, even at the cost of an interest tier. Do not let a money-scaling joker talk you out of it.
- Do not trust a high outlook on an empty board. With zero jokers and every hand at level 1, even a 300-chip Small Blind rated 98% has been lost. Treat any blind of 450 or more as below 80%, whatever the simulator says.
- Check a reading that jumps or drops with no change to the board or the blind before acting on it. Query it again, or compare it with the round player's estimate. Never let an unchecked low reading trigger a full spend.
- Before each ante step (especially 3 to 4, which is x2.8), check that the next ante's Small Blind is not already below 70%. If it is, keep buying lasting scoring even if it costs an interest tier. [seen in 15 games]
- Crisis cash discipline: when a blind or boss looks lost, every purchase must raise it clearly. That means about +10-15 points on the boss, or lifting a failing blind above about 50%. A buy that moves a doomed boss from 15% to 20% is not a fix; keep the money for the next shop and the next ante.
- When a boss reads 30-80%, do not spend more than about half your bank in one shop. Buy the single item that lifts the boss most, re-check the outlook after each purchase, and stop once the boss reads 80%+. Keep at least $10-15 going into ante 3, because rebuilt interest pays for xMult later. Crazy Joker alone lifted The Needle from 32% to 83%, but the shop went on to a reroll, Red Card and an Arcana pack ($26→$10) for a few points each, and money sat at $1-13 for the rest of the run.
- When a boss two or more blinds away reads below 30%, rank each possible buy by how much it raises that boss read per dollar. The Small and Big Blind shops are where the boss gets fixed, so look hardest there. Do not leave those shops at $6-8 and then dump $13 into the last shop before the boss. Equally, do not hold $25 through the earlier shops and then crash from $32 to $5 in one panicked shop (Jumbo, two rerolls, Campfire, Arcana, planets bought to sell for Campfire stacks that reset at the next boss).
- When a boss you must beat is below 50%, spend at most about half your money on rerolls in one shop. Stop when the next reroll would leave under about $12 for the remaining shops before the boss.
- While shops remain in the ante, never spend to $0. Keep at least the price of a common xMult ($6) plus $3 per rental due at round end, and about $5-8 for the last shop before the boss.
- Grabber, Observatory and other vouchers that do not reroll the boss, economy jokers, random Arcana or Celestial packs, off-plan levels and sideways flat-joker swaps do not count as fixes. Never put the whole bankroll on a voucher that fixes the Big Blind but leaves the boss below 20%. Director's Cut does count (see the boss rule). Once the next boss reads 10% or less, every dollar goes to rerolls, packs or planets aimed at a multiplier. Count a perishable or rental that expires this ante as already gone. Never spend your last money on a voucher and then skip a blind.
- When the final boss reads 15% or less, stop buying +5% steps and buy-then-sell trials ($1 rentals bought and sold to feed Campfire do not help). Keep $10+ for the last two shops and spend it only on what can multiply the score.
- Flag the run as in trouble as soon as the outlook shows the next ante at 0-15%. React with targeted certain upgrades in the current ante, not by emptying the bank in a panic. [seen in 13 games]
- Selling jokers, and outlook noise.
- Keep your xMult and your multiplier enablers. Never sell a permanent xMult joker (Card Sharp, The Idol, Seeing Double, a permanent x3 Flower Pot, etc.) or an engine piece (Hanging Chad with Photograph, Blueprint/Brainstorm) for a perishable or decaying joker, even if the sim says it fixes the current boss, unless no other buy fixes the blind. Hanging Chad sold for Popcorn (decayed to +4) later made Photograph look unbuyable. Do not sell an xMult for a flat or scaling +Mult joker unless the boss you are about to face is impossible with it, and never for a joker that adds no score. If one boss needs a fix, sell a flat, expiring or rental joker, or spend money. From ante 3 with no xMult, multiplier enablers (Blueprint/Brainstorm, Hanging Chad + Photograph, Polychrome) are top priority. From ante 6, the count of permanent xMult jokers should only go up (target 2+ by ante 7). Before any swap that removes a multiplier, compare the next ante's outlook with and without the swap, not just the next blind's.
- Do not sell a grown, lasting chips or Mult scaler for another flat scaler that starts near 0, or for a decaying one. Swap it only for xMult, or when the current blind would otherwise fail. Runner at +45 chips sold for Spare Trousers while The Hook already read 100%, then Trousers sold for Ice Cream when chips ran short, left a decayed Ice Cream as the main chips source at ante 5. Never sell a permanent scorer for a temporary one when the swap also drops an interest tier.
- Convert flat to multiplier when the cliff is near. If the outlook shows a boss within the next ante at 10% or less and the board has no xMult, stop guarding a current blind that reads 95%+. Sell the weakest flat joker for any xMult or scaling joker (Blackboard, Ride the Bus, Green Joker, Hanging Chad, Obelisk) as long as the current blind still reads about 75%+ after the swap.
- Do not sell a working, still-growing +Mult joker for a trigger xMult (Loyalty Card and similar) unless it fires on most boss attempts.
- In the last shop before a blind, never sell a scoring joker for another flat or conditional joker, or to nudge a later boss, if that blind's read goes down, unless it still reads 95%+ afterward. Abstract Joker loses +3 Mult for every joker sold.
- Treat outlook changes under about 10 points as noise. Never trade a known, lasting effect for a small simulated gain.
- Conditional xMult like Flower Pot is still a bonus, not the engine. Do not pick the main hand around it. When it struggles against one boss, change how you play rather than selling it.
- If your only xMult needs a hand that is hard to draw with 2 discards (The Trio, The Family, Four of a Kind), shape the deck at once with Strength, Death or Hanged Man. Keep the scorer for the hand you actually draw most until the deck can make the target hand. Otherwise stay on Pair. [seen in 12 games]
- Score the build against the actual boss, and keep a way to change bosses.
- Once the boss is shown, rate every shop of the ante against its rule, not against the Small or Big Blind. For a one-hand boss like The Needle, only per-hand +Mult, xMult and main-hand planets count. A boss debuff applies only to the boss, so play normally in the Small and Big Blinds.
- Against The Arm, never play the levelled main hand. Name one off-plan sacrifice hand, lock the round player to it, and clear in as few hands as you can. Hold main-hand planets and any Fool until after The Arm.
- Director's Cut: from ante 4, and only once the board has at least 2 lasting unconditional scorers AND the ante's ordinary blinds read about 80%+, buy it before any reroll or pack if it is in the shop and you have $10 above your interest floor. This matters most when the current or next ante's boss reads below 50%, or when the engine has a boss it cannot beat. Get it by ante 5-6 at the latest. Never buy it in antes 1-3: $10 at ante 2 on one conditional joker went unused, because cheap scorers fixed The Wall instead. It fixes only a bad boss. If an earlier blind of the ante reads below about 80%, or the next ante's ordinary blinds read below about 50% and the board has no lasting xMult, put that money into scoring, xMult hunting or main-hand planets first. Director's Cut bought for $10 while the Big Blind read 30-45%, with more held back for a Wall reroll, saw the run die on that Big Blind without reaching The Wall. Reroll a boss only when it cuts the score by about half or more (The Wall, Violet Vessel, a counter to your main hand or suit), and not if it takes you below $15 while the next ante reads under 30%. $10 to reroll The Club ($22→$12) gave The Psychic at 50% right before the ante 4 cliff.
- Buy other vouchers only if they work with the engine you already have. Observatory is dead money with Constellation. Never buy Hieroglyph or any voucher that costs a hand per round to escape an ante the board cannot beat. At Gold, 4 hands to 3 cuts the score per round by about 25% for good, and replaying an ante does not add a multiplier. Hieroglyph for $28→$18 took the replayed ante 3 Small Blind from 95-100% to 17%, and the run died; another run spent $10 of its last $15 on it at ante 5 and played the replayed ante on $0-4. Before buying any voucher with a cost, check that the next-blind read goes up.
- In any round rated below about 95%, turn off grow_joker style goals and play the highest score each hand. Do not buy fresh slow scalers (Square, Wee, Runner) at ante 3+ unless they can be fed for free. [seen in 11 games]
- In-blind pace rule: play each blind to its chip target.
- Before every hand, work out chips still needed ÷ hands left (a 300 blind over 4 hands needs 75 per hand). If the best hand you can play now won't keep that pace, discard toward a bigger hand while discards remain, even if that breaks a pair you already hold.
- With only 2 discards at Gold, use them early, while 3-4 hands remain to play what they dig up.
- On a blank board (no jokers, every hand at level 1), Pair is not the plan. A level-1 Pair scores about 30-64 and Two Pair about 60-80, both below pace. Dig for Three of a Kind (about 100-180), a Straight (about 200-260) or a Flush (about 250-300). Play a lone Pair or High Card only when it keeps pace or finishes the blind as the last hand.
- If the score after hand 2 is below 50% of the target, switch to the highest-variance line available (discard toward a Flush or Straight).
- Treat losing a blind rated 90%+ as a play error and audit it. Log every hand and discard as its own decision: cards, expected score, running total, chips still needed, hands and discards left. [seen in 2 games]
