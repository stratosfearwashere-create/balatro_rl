LESSONS FROM OUR GAMES (version 4; each seen in at least 2 games, most supported first)
- Judge a board by its reliable scoring. The outlook is simulated with the boss's effect and the build as it is, but it still averages over lucky draws: when reaching the target depends on a conditional joker (suit, rank or hand-type jokers, Flower Pot, Idol, Ancient, Castle) or on unreliable pieces, treat the boss as less safe than shown. Before each ante step (especially 3 to 4, which is x2.8), make sure the next ante's Small Blind is not already below 70% in the outlook; if it is, keep buying lasting scoring even if it costs an interest tier. [seen in 8 games]
- In antes 1-2, survival beats interest until the board has 2 lasting, unconditional scoring jokers (+Mult or chips that fire on ordinary hands) and the main hand is level 2 or higher. Until then:
- Do not leave a shop holding $15+. Spend down to about $8-10 if that is what it takes.
- Buy cheap +Mult even if it is perishable (Jolly, Raised Fist, Gros Michel as a bridge).
- Use the Yellow Deck head start on Buffoon packs.
- Reroll in the early shops of the ante, not in the pricier boss shop.
- Every joker slot and pack pick goes to scoring. Do not take utility or economy jokers (Drunkard, Chaos, Splash, Mail-In Rebate, To the Moon, Cloud 9) or a $10 voucher like Grabber.
- An early xMult (e.g. Card Sharp) must be followed at once by an unconditional base joker and a main-hand planet.
- Take a value generator only when the next boss is safe by the conservative check (2 lasting scorers in place).
The $25 hold starts at ante 3. [seen in 8 games]
- Main-hand levels are the second engine. Choose the main hand by how often you can draw it with 2 discards (check that you could draw it in 3 of 4 hands), not by how often you have played it so far. Pair is the default: it is always drawable, and Sly, Jolly and Mercury support it. Use Two Pair only for a clear reason, and Full House, Straight or Flush only with a real enabler. Commit by ante 2 and take every planet for that hand (Mercury whenever it is offered). Skip off-plan planets even when they are free. Targets: level 2-3 by the ante 2 boss (buy at least one main-hand planet before it), 3-5 by antes 3-4, 6-9 by antes 5-6. While below target, surplus above $25 goes first to main-hand planets or Celestial packs, before $6+ flat jokers, slow scalers or rerolls. Value Telescope and Planet Merchant highly. [seen in 8 games]
- Rentals by ante.
Ante 1: buy none, even at $1, unless the current blind fails without it. One exception: if you hold an xMult with no base, a big flat-chips rental (Stuntman +250) or an affordable Bootstraps is worth it.
Antes 1-3: never buy a rental just to fix a single blind.
From ante 2-3 on: one rental that is a lasting scorer and fits the main hand (Hanging Chad, a growing joker, Even Steven on an even-heavy deck) is a fine buy if you have a free slot and $28 banked.
Always:
- Never take an eternal or perishable rental, and never carry two at once.
- Sell a survival rental before any other joker once a non-rental scorer can replace it, or once the next blind is 90%+ safe without it.
- Do not let the $28/$31 floor block buys that improve the build.
- Before a boss that costs money (e.g. The Tooth), enter with cash for the cards you expect to play + $3 per rental + $5. [seen in 7 games]
- From ante 2 to ante 5, while the board has fewer than 2 multiplier sources (a suit-dependent x1.5 such as Ancient counts as half), spend all surplus above $25 in every shop. Use 1-2 $5 rerolls, Buffoon packs, and xMult or +Mult jokers. If the main hand is below its level target, buy its planet first. Never pass a real xMult (The Idol, Cavendish, Card Sharp and similar) just to keep an interest tier while a slot is open, because empty slots never grow. Joker Stencil is a stopgap: take any xMult, scaling or growing joker even if it lowers Stencil, and judge it by the score needed 1-2 antes ahead. Do not spend this surplus on Arcana, Spectral or Standard packs that add one-card tweaks, or on Golden Joker or other economy, unless you are aiming at a specific card the build needs. Spend a windfall of $20+ over target (Investment Tag, big interest) this way before the Small Blind. [seen in 6 games]
- Treat unreliable scoring as backup, not as the plan. That includes perishable jokers, decaying ones (Ice Cream, Popcorn, Seltzer), random ones (Misprint), limited-use ones (Supernova) and destroyable ones (Gros Michel, 1 in 6). Count a perishable as gone at its expiry, and start looking for a permanent replacement 2 rounds before. Replace a shrinking chips joker before it falls to about half its value. If more than half the board's scoring is unreliable, work out the bad case: Gros Michel gone, Misprint at about +5, decay after 4 more hands. Keep buying lasting scoring until the boss clears without those pieces. Never let a perishable or rental joker be your only chips source. [seen in 5 games]
- Score the build against the actual boss. Once the boss is shown, rate every shop of the ante against its rule, not against the Small or Big Blind. For a one-hand boss like The Needle, only per-hand +Mult, xMult and main-hand planets count. Skip delayed or counter jokers (Loyalty Card, Ride the Bus, Runner, Wee, Lucky Cat) and economy until the one-hand score clears. A boss debuff (e.g. The Goad on Spades) applies only to the boss, so play normally in the Small and Big Blinds. In any round rated below about 95%, turn off grow_joker style goals and play the highest score each hand. Grow scalers only on blinds that are about 100% safe. Do not buy fresh slow scalers (Square, Wee, Runner) at ante 3+ unless they can be fed for free. [seen in 4 games]
- Reroll limits:
- At most 2-3 rerolls per shop.
- In antes 1-2, never spend $15+ rerolling one shop, and stop before the next reroll would take you below $10.
- Later, stop once one shop has used about $20 on rerolls.
- Never pay $9-10 for a reroll unless you hold $60+.
Going 2-3 antes without any reroll is also a failure. While the board has fewer than 2 multipliers, use 1-2 $5 rerolls in each shop, and do it in the early shops of the ante rather than the boss shop. If the boss itself is the problem, first look for a fix aimed at that boss (Director's Cut, glass from Justice, a skip that avoids it) before rerolling more. [seen in 4 games]
- Buy only jokers that fire on the hand you actually play. Do not buy a joker for a different hand type (Droll/Crafty for Flush, Crazy for Straight, Zany for Three of a Kind, Clever for Two Pair) as a survival bridge or as filler. Buy unconditional +Mult or chips instead (Jolly, Half, Abstract, Banner, Blackboard, Popcorn), or reroll to find one. Do not play Straights or Flushes as filler without an enabler. In a desperate last shop, do not switch to a hand you can't reliably draw: with 8 cards, 2 discards and no Four Fingers or Shortcut, a Straight in one hand is only about a 25-30% event. [seen in 3 games]
- Do not skip blinds in an ante where the boss is below about 40-50%, or where the boss estimate is likely optimistic (xMult that depends on drawing specific ranks, or a boss that drains levels like The Arm). Play the last blind before the boss if it is at least 60%, and use that shop to fix the board. Rare, Uncommon, Voucher and money tags (Economy, Handy, Investment) pay out only after the boss, so skipping sends you into the boss with the same board. Skip the pre-boss blind for a money tag only if the boss has read at least 90% for two or more shops in a row and the shop you give up has nothing you want. When a Rare Tag's free joker appears, take it, selling a perishable or flat joker to make room. [seen in 3 games]
- From ante 3, do not drop below $25 ($28 with a rental) on packs or rerolls unless the next blind or the current boss is below 70% by the conservative hand check. A weak outlook for the next ante is not a reason to spend the bank now, and neither is a high outlook that rests on a variable joker (Ancient, Idol, Castle) or a boss that hides information. Spend only the surplus above the floor, on targeted buys (main-hand planets, xMult jokers), not on chains of rerolls. When the boss is below 50% and you do dip into survival money, judge each $ by how much it raises the boss estimate. Prefer joker rerolls, a main-hand planet already in the shop, or a tarot that edits trigger cards. Skip speculative Standard or Celestial packs. If two buys in a row miss, stop and keep the interest. [seen in 3 games]
- Treat Flower Pot and similar conditional xMult as a bonus, not the engine. It checks only scoring cards, so a kicker never helps. Two Pair covers all four suits only about 1 time in 6, and Full House about half the time. Do not pick the main hand around it, and do not accept an outlook that depends on it firing. Once you own a permanent x3, though, do not sell it to fix a single boss. Change how you play instead (five-card hands that cover all four suits, Wild cards). Swap it only if the replacement improves the next ante, not just the current boss. One reliable x3 is worth about +2.5 blinds. [seen in 3 games]
- In antes 1-2, do not lock a slot with an Eternal joker that is utility or will be a weak flat effect by ante 4 (Eternal Splash, Sly, Arrowhead, Fibonacci). Take one only if the next blind is below 70% without it, or if the rest of the Buffoon pack is worse and the next shop still buys real Mult. When opening a Buffoon pack with no jokers, rank the choices by how many chips they add to an ordinary Pair right now. [seen in 2 games]
