## Q1. Which archetypes are considered most reliable at Gold, and why (consistency vs ceiling)?

### Takeaway
Most community advice says pairs, two pair or high card plus generic scaling and xMult is the most consistent route at Gold, because those hands are always drawable. One experienced Gold player (nekoni, 11-win streak) disagrees outright. He prefers planet-levelled Straights and calls High Card/Pair unstable at Gold. Several sources agree that "emergent", non-forced play beats committing to one hand type early. Ceiling archetypes (PhotoChad, Baron/Mime steel kings, Triboulet, Perkeo/Observatory) are praised for raw power, but they depend on finding specific jokers.

### Cited Findings
- Steam players (Apr 2024): high card and pairs are more reliable than flush for beating Orange and Gold. Forcing pairs or high card is "often a very good option" at high stakes. Another commenter says forcing a specific strategy is the easiest way to lower your win rate. — [Steam thread 604143165271744486](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/604143165271744486) (via search summary)
- YoruHawk (says he has beaten Gold on ~5 decks), 7 Apr 2024: high card and two pair are the easiest hands to win with because they are consistent. Build-around and utility jokers are worth less at high stakes. A simple high card/pair/two pair plan is "the only consistent way" to win Orange/Gold. — [Steam thread 4356744017103053782](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4356744017103053782)
- One Steam poster calls high card "easily the best" for versatility. Another says there is no best deck because joker RNG decides runs. — [Steam thread 7410182856432960551](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/7410182856432960551) (via search summary)
- Another player calls pairs the most consistently drawable and reliably scalable hand, with better non-endless scaling than High Card. — [Steam thread 596264096371887282](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/596264096371887282) (via search summary)
- **Dissent:** nekoni (note.com, 23 Aug 2025; claims two full achievement completions and an 11-win Gold streak) says to score mainly through planet levels and treat jokers as support or multipliers. Straight is the main archetype (base score ≈ 90n² + 30n at level n). Full House suits skewed Erratic decks, and Five of a Kind suits runs with an early Ouija. He says to **avoid** High Card and Pair builds because Chips, flat Mult and xMult all have to scale together, which he calls unstable at Gold. — [nekoni guide](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Addison Kindrick (substack, 4 Jan 2025): repeat-one-hand play (e.g. Checkered flush) is beginner-friendly but less viable at higher difficulty. The strongest runs are "emergent" and adapt to the situation. He also names Obelisk/high card, Erratic five- or four-of-a-kind with Obelisk, and Ancient Joker flush as archetypes. — [Kindrick addendum](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- The Mancunion (Adam Whiteley, 1 Sep 2025): play toward the deck's strengths rather than one setup for every run. Plasma is "the easiest deck". Beating ante 8 usually needs chip, +Mult and xMult scaling (e.g. Runner, Red Card, Hologram, Constellation, Vampire), and PhotoChad is "highly rated". — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- Choost Games (Apr 2026) ranks jokers for Gold clears. S tier: Blueprint, Brainstorm, Cavendish, Triboulet, Throwback, Canio, Perkeo, DNA, Baron, Chicot. A tier: Bones, Stuntman, Four Fingers ("complete builds but can't usually carry a run alone"). — [Choost Games](https://choostgames.com/blog/balatro-jokers/) (via search summary)
- TrueAchievements guide (undated): Gold scaling is "brutal", so be pickier with jokers. Green Joker and Abstract Joker are dependable. Red Card, Ride the Bus and Supernova are "traps" that don't scale fast enough. — [TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/a424923/high-stakes-achievement) (via search summary; page returned 403)
- Unsourced claim: "people in the world who can win 50+ Gold Stake runs in a row". — [nekoni guide](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Majority view: the generic pair/two-pair/high-card shell is the most consistent, and narrow ceiling builds are opportunistic. That matches DrSpectred's "default pairs" and "generic beats narrow".
- The strongest documented counter-philosophy is planet-levelling a single larger hand (Straight). It trades joker dependence for a reliance on Planet/Telescope/Observatory supply.

### Gaps
- Win-rate data per archetype at Gold could not be found. All reliability claims are anecdotal.
- The content of the DrSpectred and Balatro University videos (e.g. "PAIRS ARE BANNED" streak streams, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ68t7ZGbzM) was not retrieved.

## Q2. What scores per hand are needed by ante, and what combos reach them?

### Takeaway
Gold uses Purple-stake scaling (1.0.1 values). The base, which is the small blind, is 300 / 1,000 / 3,200 / 9,000 / 25,000 / 60,000 / 110,000 / 200,000 for antes 1 to 8. The big blind is ×1.5 and the boss ×2, so the **ante 8 boss is 400k** and Violet Vessel is 1.2M. To one-shot, you need roughly 10⁴ by ante 4, around 10⁵ by ante 6 and around 4×10⁵ to 10⁶ at ante 8. Over 4 hands, the ante 8 boss needs about 10⁵ per hand.

### Cited Findings
- Purple-and-up base per ante: 300, 1,000, 3,200, 9,000, 25,000, 60,000, 110,000, 200,000. Green-and-up: 300, 900, 2,600, 8,000, 20,000, 36,000, 60,000, 100,000. Plasma doubles these. Endless Purple: ante 9 is 460k, ante 10 is 2.2M, ante 11 is 29M, ante 12 is 1.2e9. — [Balatro wiki, Blinds and Antes](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Blinds_and_Antes)
- Big blind = 1.5× small and most bosses = 2× small, with a few exceptions. — [Steam thread 596263353496658219](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/596263353496658219) (via search summary); [LIACS thesis](https://theses.liacs.nl/pdf/2025-2026-WiskerJ.pdf)
- 1.0.1f changed Purple scaling: ante 6 went from 50k to 60k, ante 7 from 90k to 110k and ante 8 from 180k to 200k. Earlier antes got easier (ante 4 went from 10k to 9k). — [1.0.1f notes (press-start)](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)
- nekoni: the final Gold target is 400,000, and Violet Vessel is 1,200,000. Example: a high-level hand with base 1470 chips × 148 mult scores 217,560 with no jokers, enough for two hands. His level targets are hand level 2–3 by ante 2, 6–9 by antes 5–6 and ~12 by ante 7. — [nekoni guide](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Kindrick: successful runs exceed 600 mult through xMult jokers, Chariot (steel) and polychrome. Chips have no exponential scaling, but enough planets and Hierophants (bonus cards) cover late chip needs. — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- **Error to flag:** Two Average Gamers (29 Jan 2026) says the ante 8 boss is "roughly 300 million chips". That contradicts the wiki table, which gives 400k at Gold. — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
- PhotoChad: leading with a face card gives "at least x8" from two common jokers (Photograph x2, with Hanging Chad retriggering the first card twice, so x2³). — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/); nekoni also cites ~8× for Hanging Chad + Photograph/glass — [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Baron + Mime with steel kings: one guide estimates five kings held give ~x7.6 from Baron alone and ~x11 with Mime. Other sources give different figures. — [games.gg Baron/Mime guide](https://games.gg/balatro/guides/baron-mime-balatro-build/) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- Per-blind targets at Gold (from the wiki base × 1/1.5/2):
  - Ante 4: 9k / 13.5k / 18k
  - Ante 6: 60k / 90k / 120k
  - Ante 7: 110k / 165k / 220k
  - Ante 8: 200k / 300k / 400k (Violet Vessel 1.2M)
  - With 4 hands that is ~5k per hand at the ante 4 boss, ~30k at the ante 6 boss and ~100k at the ante 8 boss. A one-shot needs 4× those.
- Ante 5 to 6 is the steepest step: the base goes from 25k to 60k (×2.4) after 1.0.1f raised it. That supports DrSpectred's "ante 6 is the wall".
- Rough arithmetic for a pair board **[game knowledge, unverified here]**: a level-5 pair (~70+ chips, ~10 mult base) plus ~+30 mult from a scaled Ride the Bus/flat mult, plus ~150 chips from Square/Runner/Hiker or card chips, gives ~220 chips × ~40 mult ≈ 9k. That is ante 4 to 5 territory. One x3 joker gives ~27k (ante 6 small and big), and two x3s give ~80k (ante 7 to 8 per hand). That is consistent with "one scaling source plus one x3" sufficing through ante 6 to 7, and needing more xMult or a bigger base for the 400k boss.

### Gaps
- No source gave measured score-per-hand curves from real Gold winning runs (e.g. median best hand at antes 4, 6 and 8).

## Q3. Concrete transition rules (selling scaling +mult for xMult, stopping chips, deck thinning)

### Takeaway
The sources broadly agree on a timeline:
- Economy and interest cap first: $25 by antes 2 to 4.
- One or two flat or scaling +Mult jokers for antes 1 to 4.
- Add xMult from about antes 5 to 6. Sell economy jokers there too.
- By ante 8 every slot should score, as roughly 1 chip source + 1 or 2 +Mult + xMult for the rest, with xMult on the right.

Explicit "sell the scaling +mult for xMult" rules are less common. More often the advice is to sell economy jokers and conditional or hand-specific jokers. One source says flat +Mult is no longer needed by ante 7.

### Cited Findings
- Steam/community: in the endgame it's "almost always better" to have ONE good +mult joker and ONE +chips source, with xMult for every other slot. — search-result quote from [Steam thread 4356744017098574801](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4356744017098574801/?l=german) / related threads
- A Gold guide recommends one chip joker, one or two +Mult jokers, and xMult for the rest. Put xMult furthest right, because jokers trigger left to right. — search summary of [Steam thread 7252559508585171872](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/7252559508585171872)
- Two Average Gamers (Jan 2026):
  - Early economy jokers (Cloud 9, To the Moon, Golden Joker).
  - $25 interest cap by ante 3.
  - Antes 5 to 6: sell weak economy jokers for scaling.
  - By ante 8 every slot contributes.
  - Chips and flat mult matter less after ~ante 5.
  - Stack xMult on the right.
  - Conditional or non-scaling hand-specific jokers (e.g. Lusty, Wrathful) become dead slots after ante 4.
  - [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
- nekoni (Aug 2025):
  - Additive mult (Jolly) is fine early.
  - Add an xMult (e.g. Photograph) at antes 5 to 6.
  - By ante 7 "only Mult-multipliers and Chips are needed". Collect extra xMult (Flower Pot, Ancient, Bloodstone) for the final boss.
  - Sell economy jokers by antes 5 to 6.
  - Take at most one Rental, from ante 5 on.
  - Sell Burglar before discard-forcing bosses and Matador right after its boss.
  - Sacrifice (Spectral) is strong early for $20 plus deck thinning. Death and Tarots raise counts of a rank.
  - [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Mancunion (Sep 2025):
  - Reach the interest cap around rounds 3 to 4 and stay there.
  - Avoid rentals until the economy is stable.
  - Perishables are acceptable within five rounds of the ante 8 boss.
  - Runner's straight requirement can be dropped later for another hand type.
  - Plasma can pivot from chips to xMult.
  - [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- Kindrick: aim for $15–20 left at the end of each shop so the next shop starts at $20 or more. One-shot early blinds for the remaining-hand bonus. Rocket is an exception to "sell money jokers late". Flash Card's permanent scaling is underrated next to Bootstraps, Swashbuckler, Green Joker, Supernova and Ride the Bus, which he calls riskier. — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- YoruHawk (Apr 2024): packs cost more at higher stakes, so buy them early. Avoid heavy rerolls and never drop to $0. Prefer sellable guaranteed-money jokers (Gold Joker, Cloud 9). — [Steam 4356744017103053782](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4356744017103053782). Note: 1.0.1f removed Orange's pack-price increase ([patch notes](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)), so his pack-cost reasoning is outdated.
- DonDarker (Apr 2024), Orange win with a Checkered deck: levelled flushes just enough early, then switched to a high card/pair build. — [Steam 4356744017103053782](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4356744017103053782)
- Ride the Bus face-card risk: thin face cards out with Hanged Man so an accidental face card doesn't reset it. — search summaries ([TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/a424923/high-stakes-achievement), [Steam 596264096371887282](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/596264096371887282))
- High xMult on low base mult performs poorly, so check +Mult before committing to a rare xMult. This comes from a low-quality SEO page and should be treated cautiously. — [sdp.otoy.com](https://sdp.otoy.com/best-xmult-jokers-balatro)

### Inferences
- The consensus sale order is: economy jokers first (antes 5 to 6), then conditional or hand-specific flat jokers (after ante 4), then flat +Mult once two or more xMult are online (ante 7+, per nekoni).
- "Keep exactly one +Mult and one chip source" is the most-cited endgame rule. It differs from DrSpectred's "one scaling source plus small flat support" mainly in timing, not substance.
- No source gave a numeric "stop buying chips" rule. The closest is "chips/flat mult matter less after ~ante 5" (Two Average Gamers) and Kindrick's view that planets plus bonus cards cover late chips.

### Gaps
- No quantitative threshold was found for selling a scaled Ride the Bus or Green Joker (e.g. "sell at +N mult when an x3 appears").
- Deck-thinning timing (when to start Hanged Man or Trading Card) is only mentioned generically. No ante-specific rule was found.

## Q4. Per-archetype dossiers (key pieces, enablers, start time, failure modes, must-have vs nice)

### Takeaway
The sources cover the major archetypes unevenly. Below, sourced facts are cited and anything else is marked **[game knowledge, unverified here]**. Joker values reflect the 1.0.1f balance pass, which also changed several archetype-relevant jokers.

### Cited Findings

**Patch changes relevant to archetypes (1.0.1f, May 2024; the current major version as of these sources):**
- Hanging Chad: retrigger first card 1× → 2×
- Vampire: X0.2 → X0.1 per enhancement, and now only eats scoring cards' enhancements
- Steel Joker: X0.25 → X0.2
- Lucky Cat: X0.2 → X0.25
- Campfire: X0.5 → X0.25
- Glass Joker: X0.5 → X0.75
- Bloodstone: 1/3 for X2 → 1/2 for X1.5
- Square Joker: starts +16 → +0 chips, costs $4
- Wee Joker: starts at +0
- Sinful jokers: +4 → +3
- Banner: +40 → +30 per discard
- Hiker: +4 → +5
- Smiley: +4 → +5
- Midas Mask: now only applies to scoring face cards
- Madness: no longer triggers on boss blinds
- Ancient: suit can't repeat
- Swashbuckler: counts all jokers
- Vagabond: becomes rare, triggers at ≤$4
- Stuntman: +300 → +250 chips, becomes rare
- Seance and Sixth Sense: become uncommon
- Gros Michel: 1/4 → 1/6
- Saturn: +2 → +3 mult
- Gold Stake: the −1 hand size was replaced by Rental jokers ($3/round)
- Orange: gains Perishables, loses pack-price increases
- Source: [1.0.1f notes](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)
- Obelisk was nerfed from X0.5 to X0.2 between the demo and release. To use it, the most-played hand should exceed every other hand's play count by about 10. — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)

**Pairs / two pair / high card with scaling jokers (the default shell)**
- Pair-build complements: Mime and/or Baron (with steel kings). Two or three xMult jokers such as Blackboard, Cavendish, Card Sharp and Vampire. — [Steam 604143165271744486](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/604143165271744486) (via search summary)
- Blue-deck pair jokers: Green Joker, Ride the Bus, Supernova, Space Joker, Runner, Square Joker, To Do List, Card Sharp, Vagabond. — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- Early Gold options: Half Joker + Banner for high card or pair, and Delayed Gratification pairs for money. — [Steam 7410182856432960551](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/7410182856432960551) (via search summary)
- Ride the Bus is disputed. Some guides call it "amazing"; TrueAchievements calls it a Gold "trap". — [TrueAchievements](https://www.trueachievements.com/a424923/high-stakes-achievement) (via search summary)
- Square Joker triggers only on exactly 4 cards, so a plain pair does not feed it. Two pair, or a pair plus 2 kickers, does. — search summary noting a guide's error ([Steam 596264096371887282](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/596264096371887282))
- Failure modes **[game knowledge, unverified here]**:
  - The Psychic (needs 5 cards) and The Eye (no repeat hand type) hurt one-hand spam.
  - The Flint halves base chips and mult.
  - Verdant Leaf, The Plant and The Mark hurt face-based xMult.
  - Violet Vessel (6× base) is the hardest ante 8 finisher.

**Flush**
- Checkered deck flush plus suit jokers (Lusty, Bloodstone, Oops! All 6s; Wrathful, Arrowhead). Straight-flush support: Shortcut, Four Fingers, Seance. — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- Ancient Joker with levelled flush and Lovers tarots. Ancient becomes more reliable with retriggers (Seltzer, Sock & Buskin, Hack). — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- One-hand flush play is called less viable at high difficulty. — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- Sinful jokers were nerfed to +3. — [patch notes](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)
- Failure modes **[game knowledge, unverified here]**: suit-debuff bosses (The Club, Goad, Window, Head) and The Psychic is irrelevant here. Smaller effective draws at Gold are a further problem.

**Straights (planet-levelling)**
- nekoni's primary build:
  - Planets to hand level ~6–9 by antes 5 to 6 and ~12 by ante 7.
  - One Mult joker plus economy by ante 2, about two Mult jokers by antes 3 to 4.
  - xMult (Photograph, Flower Pot, Ancient, Bloodstone) for antes 5 to 8.
  - Hand-size and discard support (Juggler, Turtle Bean, Troubadour, Drunkard, Merry Andy, Paintbrush, Wasteful).
  - [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Perkeo + Observatory + Saturn: negative planet copies and X1.5 per held matching planet. — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
- Saturn was buffed from +2 to +3 mult. — [patch notes](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)
- Runner can be dropped later for another hand type. Abandoned deck suits straights, four of a kind, Hack, Fibonacci and Ride the Bus. — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)

**Four of a kind / rank-uniform / Five of a kind**
- Erratic deck with 8 or more of one rank plus Obelisk to one-shot early antes. — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- Five of a Kind when Ouija appears at antes 1 to 2. Full House on skewed Erratic decks. — [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Abandoned deck supports four of a kind. — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)

**Glass / steel with retriggers (PhotoChad, Baron/Mime, Sock & Buskin)**
- PhotoChad is "highly rated". — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- PhotoChad gives at least x8 from two commons, provided a face card is played first. — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
- Hanging Chad + Photograph or glass gives ~8×. Glass cards serve as a late-game multiplier, and their before-joker timing matters less at high hand levels. Steel is best on Blue or Red Seal cards. — [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Hanging Chad's buff from 1× to 2× is what makes PhotoChad work. — [patch notes](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)
- Baron + Mime steel kings:
  - Red-seal steel kings, with Mime retriggering held effects.
  - DNA or Steel Joker to grow the deck. Blueprint or Brainstorm copy Baron or Mime.
  - High card early to keep four kings in hand. Money jokers to reroll for Baron.
  - Players disagree: one found it "not that great at higher stakes", another reached ante 18.
  - [games.gg](https://games.gg/balatro/guides/baron-mime-balatro-build/), [Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/604152945255155798) (via search summary)
- **Error to flag:** one search-summarised source says Hanging Chad retriggers held-in-hand effects. That is wrong: Mime does that, and Hanging Chad retriggers the first *played* scoring card.
- Triboulet + Sock & Buskin: x2 per K/Q, with played face cards retriggered. — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
- Failure modes **[game knowledge, unverified here]**:
  - Face-card debuffs (The Plant) and face-down cards (The Mark) shut off Photograph, Baron and Sock & Buskin.
  - Glass breaks at 1 in 4.
  - The Pillar and The Tooth are general bosses that also hurt.
  - Steel Joker was nerfed to X0.2.

**xMult engines**
- Mancunion (ante-8 needs): Runner, Red Card, Hologram, Constellation, Vampire. Copy your best joker with Blueprint or Brainstorm. — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- Scaling xMult values: Hologram X0.25 per card added, Throwback X0.25 per skip, Vampire eats enhancements, Red Card +3 per skipped pack. — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
- Patched values: Vampire X0.1, Lucky Cat X0.25, Campfire X0.25, Glass Joker X0.75, Steel Joker X0.2, Bloodstone 1/2 for X1.5; Madness no longer grows on bosses. — [patch notes](https://press-start.com.au/news/2024/04/08/the-latest-balatro-update-has-some-massive-changes-that-should-make-winning-easier/)
- Cavendish and Throwback are S tier for Gold. — [Choost](https://choostgames.com/blog/balatro-jokers/) (via search summary)
- Static xMult values **[game knowledge, unverified here]**:
  - Cavendish X3 (1/1000 destruction)
  - Card Sharp X3 on a repeat hand
  - Blackboard X3 if all held cards are spades or clubs
  - Flower Pot X3 with all four suits scoring
  - The Duo X2, Trio X3, Family X4, Order X3, Tribe X2
  - Constellation X0.1 per planet
  - Obelisk X0.2 per non-most-played hand

**Copy jokers**

## Q5. How players commit to or pivot from early shop offers, and typical joker slot allocation at ante 8

### Takeaway
Players commit lightly. They take whatever early flat or scaling +Mult and economy the shop offers (Jolly, Half, Banner, Green, Ride the Bus, Cloud 9, Golden) and keep the hand type flexible. Some level a hand early "just enough" and then switch to high card/pair. The typical ante 8 board in written guidance is **1 chip source + 1 (to 2) +Mult + 2 to 3 xMult**, xMult on the right. A copy joker on the best xMult is a common upgrade. Posted Gold board screenshots could not be retrieved, so no concrete boards are available.

### Cited Findings
- DonDarker (Apr 2024): levelled flushes just enough early on, then moved to high card/pair. — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4356744017103053782)
- Ante 1 targets: Orbital tag for the main hand, Top-up tag, a chip or mult joker, or a planet. Jolly Joker can carry pairs and two pair early. — [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Black deck (+1 slot, −1 hand): take short-term +Mult (Half Joker, Popcorn) plus economy (Cloud 9, Golden, Rocket). Restart for an Investment tag. — [Mancunion](https://mancunion.com/2025/09/01/a-guide-to-gold-stake-balatro/)
- Slot allocation at ante 8:
  - 1 chip + 1 or 2 +Mult + xMult for the rest. — search-summary quotes from Steam threads ([7252559508585171872](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/7252559508585171872); [4356744017098574801](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/4356744017098574801/?l=german))
  - Every slot contributes, with xMult on the right. — [Two Average Gamers](https://www.twoaveragegamers.com/balatro-strategy-guide-the-joker-combos-that-actually-win-runs/)
  - nekoni: "only Mult-multipliers and Chips" by ante 7, with planets as the base. At ante 8, sell surplus xMult (unless the boss is Violet Vessel) to gold-sticker other jokers. — [nekoni](https://note.com/teriburi/n/n9b9310f3f46b?hl=en)
- Non-scoring utility jokers are hard to justify in 5 slots. — [Kindrick](https://addisonkindrick.substack.com/p/10-balatro-tier-list-addendum)
- Anecdote: "all you need to get to ante 12 is any normal build plus some steel and/or glass cards". — [Steam 7410182856432960551](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/7410182856432960551) (via search summary)
- Anecdote: "my main hand was pairs, at least at the end", from a player who beat Gold. — [Steam 604143165271744486](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2379780/discussions/0/604143165271744486) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- **Where sources agree or disagree with DrSpectred's rules:**
  - **Default pairs:** most Steam players and YoruHawk agree. nekoni disagrees outright (Straights via planets, avoid Pair/High Card). Kindrick prefers "emergent" play over any default.
  - **One scaling source plus small flat support, and xMult needed only at antes 7 to 8:** mostly agrees. The "1 chip + 1 +Mult + rest xMult" endgame rule fits. nekoni puts the first xMult earlier (antes 5 to 6) and drops flat mult entirely by ante 7. Two Average Gamers also says the switch to scaling and xMult comes at antes 5 to 6.
  - **Ante 6 is the wall:** supported by the scaling table, where ante 5 to 6 is ×2.4 after the 1.0.1f increase. No source states it in words.
  - **Ride the Bus plus Square beats ante 6:** contested. TrueAchievements calls Ride the Bus a Gold "trap", Kindrick calls it riskier than Flash Card, and the Mancunion lists it as a Blue-deck pair joker. Square needs exactly 4 cards, so it fits two pair or a pair with kickers, not a bare pair.
  - **One x3 beats ante 8:** not directly addressed. Mancunion and nekoni both talk about stacking several xMult or scaling jokers for ante 8, and nekoni collects extra xMult for the final boss. That suggests other sources treat one x3 as thinner than DrSpectred implies, unless the base is large (planet levels).
  - **Retriggers rarely have targets except Hanging Chad:** partly agrees. PhotoChad is widely praised (Mancunion "highly rated", Two Average Gamers, nekoni), consistent with Chad being the exception. However, Baron/Mime steel kings and Triboulet/Sock & Buskin are cited as winning combos, and Kindrick notes Seltzer, Sock & Buskin and Hack make Ancient more reliable.
  - **Generic beats narrow:** strong agreement. Forcing a strategy lowers win rate (Steam); utility and build-around jokers are worth less at high stakes (YoruHawk); conditional jokers become dead slots after ante 4 (Two Average Gamers); "emergent" play wins (Kindrick).
